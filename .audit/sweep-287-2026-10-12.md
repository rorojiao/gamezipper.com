## R898 ad CSS alignment verification (the primary purpose of this sweep)

R898 (commit d8039ce046) aligned the page-CSS for gz-ad-above-game and gz-ad-below-canvas divs to match the monetag bannerCSS values that `monetag-manager.js` rewrites at L1991 (`existing.style.cssText = bannerCSS`).
