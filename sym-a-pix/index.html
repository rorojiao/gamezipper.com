How To Play Sym-a-Pix

You get an N×N grid where every cell shows a clue (0–9). Each clue tells you how many of its 8 surrounding neighbors must be filled.

  • Click a cell to fill it (turn it on). Right-click or long-press to clear it.
  • Clue 0: all 8 neighbors must be empty (the cell itself can be either).
  • Clue 9: all 8 neighbors must be filled.
  • Cross-reference adjacent clues: if clue A says "3 filled" and clue B (overlapping 5 cells with A) says "5 filled", you can deduce which cells are uniquely determined.
  • The grid must be point-symmetric: cell (r,c) equals cell (N-1-r, N-1-c). Use this to halve your work!
  • The puzzle is solved when every clue matches AND the grid is symmetric.

Controls:

  • Click / tap — fill a cell.
  • Right-click / long-press — clear a cell.
  • Enter check your solution.
  • R restart the level.
  • H reveal one correct cell (3 hints per level).

Each puzzle has exactly one unique solution. Find it!
