Tilt Maze — Roll Balls Into Holes Logic Puzzle

TILT MAZE
Roll every ball into a hole
Tilt the grid in any of four directions. Every ball rolls until it hits a wall, another ball, or falls into a hole. Plan your tilts carefully — one wrong tilt and you may be stuck!
30 unique-solution puzzles
5 difficulty tiers · arrow keys / on-screen pad

HOW TO PLAY

  • • Tilt the grid using arrow keys, WASD, or the on-screen direction pad.
  • • Each tilt rolls every ball in that direction simultaneously.
  • • A ball rolls until it hits a wall, another ball, or falls into a hole.
  • • A tilt that pushes a ball off the grid edge (not into a hole) is invalid and undoes itself.
  • • Goal: roll every ball into a hole. Win when no balls remain on the grid.
  • • Use HINT to see the next tilt in the solution.
  • • Use UNDO to reverse your last tilt.
  • • Each puzzle has exactly one solution.
