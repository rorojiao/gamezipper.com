How to Play Hexa Bridges

Goal: Draw bridges between adjacent hex cells to form a single connected tree.

Clues: Each numbered cell must have exactly that many bridges meeting it (degree = clue number).

Empty cells: May have 0, 1, or 2 bridges.

Tree rule: All bridges together form one connected group with no loops. Every bridge is reachable from every other.

Controls: Tap two adjacent hexes to draw a bridge; tap again to remove. Hint reveals one correct bridge (3 per level). Undo reverts last action. Check counts errors without revealing the solution.
