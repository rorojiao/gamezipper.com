Circular Color Puzzle

Color the Petals

Each puzzle shows a ring of 12 petals arranged around a center. Assign each petal one of 4 colors (red, teal, gold, blue). Each color must be used exactly 3 times. Two petals at ring-distance 1, 2, or 3 may not share a color. Some petals are pre-colored as anchors — fill the rest to bloom the unique rosette.

•Tap an empty petal to choose a color from the picker.
•Each color (R / T / G / B) is used exactly 3 times across the ring.
•Adjacency rule: no two petals within ring-distance 1, 2, or 3 may share a color.
•Anchor petals are pre-colored and cannot be changed.

How to Play

Tap any cell to toggle it between empty and marked. The number inside each colored region tells you how many marked cells that region must contain.

Mark cells so every region has its clue count AND all marked cells form a single orthogonally connected path (you can walk from any marked cell to any other via up/down/left/right neighbors).

Press Hint to reveal one correct cell (you have 3 hints per level). Undo reverts your last change. Reset clears the level.

Use the arrows to navigate levels.
