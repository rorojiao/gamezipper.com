How To Play Magnets

Fill every cell of the grid with a positive (+) or negative (−) magnetic pole.

  • Red ✕ between two cells = those cells must be different poles.
  • Blue = between two cells = those cells must be the same pole.
  • Numbers along the top and left are row / column clues: the count of + poles in that row or column.
  • Cells highlighted at the start are fixed poles — you can't change them.

Controls:

  • Click a cell to cycle: empty → + → − → empty.
  • + key places a + pole in the next empty cell.
  • − key places a − pole in the next empty cell.
  • Enter check your solution.
  • R restart the level.
  • H reveal one correct cell (3 hints per level).
  • Esc back to menu.

Each puzzle has exactly one unique solution. Find it!
