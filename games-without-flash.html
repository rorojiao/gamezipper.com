No-Flash Games FAQ

What happened to Flash Player games?

Adobe killed Flash Player on December 31, 2020. Browsers removed the plugin and old Flash sites stopped loading. HTML5 is the successor — it's built into every modern browser, no install required.

Do these games need any plugin at all?

No. Pure HTML5 and JavaScript. Open the page, click play. That's it.

I miss old Flash games — what should I play?

Try T-Rex Runner (endless runners), Tetris and Pong (faithful classics), Bubble Shooter (aim-and-pop), or Slope (3D speed). The genres live on, just without the plugin.

Will they work on a school Chromebook?

Yes — HTML5 games run perfectly on Chromebooks and are touch-friendly on mobile too.
