Why Lights Out?

Lights Out is a 1995 Tiger Electronics handheld classic. The puzzle looks simple: a 5×5 grid of lights, click any cell to flip a cross. But the cascading toggles create deep logic chains — a single tap can ripple across the entire board.

The math behind Lights Out: the toggle matrix over GF(2) has rank 23 with a 2-dimensional kernel, so every solvable state has exactly 4 equivalent solutions (one per coset). We pick the canonical “smallest” solution as the unique answer.

Lights Out — Rules

Lights Out is a classic puzzle. The grid has some lights on and some off.

Tap any cell to toggle that cell and its 4 neighbors (up, down, left, right). Diagonal neighbors are unaffected.

Goal: all lights off.

Buttons:
• Hint (3/level): reveals a correct tap with a glow.
• Check: validates the current state.
• Restart: reloads the level.
