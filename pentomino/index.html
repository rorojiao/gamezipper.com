PENTOMINOES
Fill the outline with 12 classic pentominoes
The 12 free pentominoes are flat shapes of 5 connected squares, named F, I, L, N, P, T, U, V, W, X, Y, Z.
Each level gives you an outline shape and a set of pieces — fit them all in without overlap.

5 difficulty tiers · 12 pentominoes

HOW TO PLAY

  • • Tap a piece in the side tray to select it.
  • • Tap a grid cell to place the piece (top-left aligned to that cell).
  • • Tap the piece in the tray again to cycle rotation/reflection.
  • • Tap an already-placed piece on the grid to remove it.
  • • Hit CHECK when every outline cell is filled.
  • • Use HINT if stuck — reveals one piece placement.
  • • Each puzzle has exactly one solution.
