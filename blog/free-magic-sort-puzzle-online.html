What Is Magic Sort?

Magic Sort is a sorting puzzle where you have several bottles filled with layers of colored water, and your job is to sort them so each bottle contains only one color. The catch: you can only pour the top layer of any bottle, and you can only pour it onto a same-color layer or into an empty bottle.

It sounds trivially simple. By level 10 you're juggling 6 colors and 2 empty bottles. By level 100 you're planning 8 moves ahead, working around a tight space budget, and trying not to paint yourself into a corner by filling the last empty bottle with mixed layers. By level 5,000 — well, that's where Magic Sort gets interesting.

How to Play Magic Sort

The rules fit in two sentences. Mastering them takes longer than you'd think:

  1. Tap a bottle — The top colored water layer will lift, ready to pour.
  2. Tap another bottle — The water pours in. You can only pour onto a same-color layer, or into an empty bottle.
  3. Complete a bottle — Fill a bottle with 4 layers of the same color to clear it (or one full set in higher-difficulty tiers).
  4. Win the level — When every bottle contains only one color, you win. Stars are awarded based on how few extra moves you used.
  5. Use undo if you get stuck — GameZipper's Magic Sort has an unlimited undo button, so there's no penalty for experimentation. There's also an extra-tube power-up if you truly run out of space.

Strategy: How to Actually Beat Hard Levels

Casual players sort reactively. Expert players plan the empty-bottle budget first. Here's the framework:

  1. Count colors and bottles. If you have N colors, you need at least N bottles (one per color) plus 1–2 empty bottles to maneuver. If you have only N bottles total, you're in trouble.
  2. Find the easiest complete bottle first. Look for a color that already has 3 of 4 layers in one place. Pour the 4th to complete it — now you have a free bottle.
  3. Never pour into a bottle unless it makes the bottle more sorted. Every pour that creates a mixed-color bottle is "debt" you'll have to repay later.
  4. Save the undo button. Undo is precious on hard levels. Plan two moves ahead before tapping.
  5. If you have an extra-tube power-up, save it for level 50+ when the bottle budget gets tight.
