How To Play Tatami

Tile the grid with 1×2 or 2×1 rectangles so that every cell is covered.

  • Gold cells are anchors — they belong to a pre-placed tile. Don't disturb them.
  • No four tiles may meet at any corner. Wherever four cells meet at a single point, exactly two tiles must share that corner (not four).
  • Click an empty cell to start a new tile, then click the partner cell (adjacent horizontally or vertically) to place it.
  • Click an existing non-anchor tile to remove it.

Controls:

  • Click an empty cell, then a neighbor — place a 1×2 or 2×1 tile.
  • Click an existing tile — remove it.
  • Esc — cancel the current placement.
  • Enter check your solution.
  • R restart the level.
  • H reveal one correct tile (3 hints per level).

Each puzzle has exactly one unique solution. Find it!
