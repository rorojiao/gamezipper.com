How To Play Dot-a-Pix

You're shown an N×N grid. Some cells contain blue anchor dots that are already revealed (these are part of the hidden picture and cannot be changed). Other cells are empty.

Your job: tap empty cells to add a pink dot. When you've correctly placed all the dots, the grid reveals a hidden picture — a recognizable image like a heart, star, rocket, butterfly, or animal.

How to solve:

  • Look at the anchor dots — they're a partial preview of the picture.
  • Use the anchor pattern to deduce where the remaining dots should go.
  • Tap any empty cell to add a dot. Tap your dot to remove it.
  • Tap an anchor dot to remove it — impossible. Anchors are locked.
  • The puzzle is solved when every dot in the grid matches the target picture.

Controls:

  • Click / tap — toggle a dot (add if empty, remove if filled).
  • H — reveal one correct dot (3 hints per level).
  • U — undo your last move.
  • R — restart the level.
  • Esc — back to level select.

Scoring: 3 stars = no hints used; 2 stars = 1 hint; 1 star = 2-3 hints. Lower times = better!
