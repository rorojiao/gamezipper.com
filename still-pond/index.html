Build a Still-Life

Each puzzle shows a grid with some cells already live (yellow) or empty (gray). Tap any blank cell to flip between empty and filled. Your goal: make the entire grid a still-life — a configuration that, after one GoL step, looks exactly the same.

•GoL rule: Live cells with 2 or 3 live neighbors survive. Dead cells with exactly 3 live neighbors are born. Everything else dies.
•Goal: A still-life is a grid where applying GoL once produces the same grid.
•Tip: Look for blocks (2×2 filled squares), tubs (4 cells in a + shape), and ponds (8 cells in a hollow 4×4).

How to Play

Tap any cell that is not given (yellow or gray cells with a locked feel). Each tap flips that cell between empty and filled.

Use the Check button to see how many cells violate the still-life rule. The goal is zero violations — when the entire grid is a still-life, you win.

Press Hint to reveal one correct cell (you have 3 hints per level). Undo reverts your last change. Reset clears your progress on this level.

Use the arrows to navigate levels.
