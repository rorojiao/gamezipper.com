How to Play Matchstick Puzzle

Matchstick Puzzle is a brain-teaser where you fix broken math equations or transform shapes by moving a limited number of matchsticks. Each level shows a digital-style equation (like "6 + 4 = 10") or a shape made of sticks. To move a stick, drag it to a new position — it snaps to the grid. The level is solved when the equation becomes mathematically correct, or the shape matches the target. The HUD shows your moves used versus the allowed moves (1 or 2 per level). Solve in the minimum number of moves to earn 3 stars.

Tips & Strategy

Learn the stick anatomy of each digit — a "6" uses 6 sticks, a "1" uses only 2, and a "+" shares a vertical stick with the digits beside it. The trick is usually converting one digit into another by relocating a single stick: 6↔9, 5↔3, 8↔0/6/9, 2↔3, or even changing an operator (+ ↔ -). For shape levels, count the target figure's sticks and identify which current sticks can relocate to create the new edges. Use Undo freely — it doesn't cost moves. The Hint button reveals the level's intended approach without giving away the exact solution.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this game free?

Yes! Matchstick Puzzle is completely free to play in your browser — no download or sign-up required.

Can I play on mobile?

Yes — drag matchsticks with your finger. Fully touch-optimized for phones and tablets.

How many levels?

30 levels across 3 tiers. Tier 1 (levels 1–10) covers single-move equation fixes; Tier 2 (11–19) requires two-move equations; Tier 3 (20–30) challenges you to transform shapes (squares, triangles, fish, house, hourglass, diamond).

Can I undo a move?

Yes — tap the Undo button to revert your last stick move. Undo does not count against your move budget, so experiment freely.
