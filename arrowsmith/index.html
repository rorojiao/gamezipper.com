How To Play Arrowsmith

You get an N×N grid where every cell shows a clue number (0–4). That number tells you how many of the cell's 4 cardinal neighbors (N/S/E/W) have arrows pointing AT it.

  • Click an empty cell to cycle through arrows: North → East → South → West → empty.
  • Anchor arrows (shown in muted color) are pre-filled and cannot be changed.
  • Cross-reference neighboring clues: if clue A says "2 arrows point at me" and clue B (sharing 2 neighbors with A) says "1 arrow points at me", you can deduce which neighbor arrows are forced.
  • The puzzle is solved when every clue number matches AND every cell is filled.
  • Each puzzle has exactly one unique solution. Find it!

Controls:

  • Click / tap — cycle arrow direction (N → E → S → W → empty).
  • Enter — check your solution.
  • R — restart the level.
  • H — reveal one correct arrow (3 hints per level).
  • Esc — back to level select.

Scoring: 3 stars for fast solve with no hints. Fewer hints & lower time = more stars.
